# HUMAN.md

Status: Human context refresher
Purpose: Help agents understand how the Human may communicate during Aura Lab work

This file is context, not project authority.

It does not replace `AGENTS.md`, `workspace/current.md`, `workspace/critical/`, accepted handoffs, or durable docs. It does not authorize Dev work, live/private/destructive actions, shared-file edits, product-direction changes, or target-project adoption.

## Communication Shape

The Human may guide Aura Lab through discussion, preference, metaphor, reaction, examples, exploratory language, or developing vision rather than formal command language.

Treat Human discussion as meaningful. Do not ignore it because it is informal.

Also do not treat every Human sentence as automatic authorization. Sort discussion into intent, advisory context, accepted direction, parked ideas, unresolved questions, and possible future runway only when the role workflow or Human signal calls for that.

## Cooperative Default

Human conversation is advisory and cooperative unless explicitly framed as a decision, approval, instruction, or runway request.

Brief affirmations such as "sounds good", "nice", "yeah", or similar may indicate agreement, encouragement, or permission to continue a clearly stated role-appropriate next action. They are not broad authorization to implement, change authority, accept doctrine, broaden scope, edit shared/global files, or touch target projects.

If an affirmation follows a specific proposed action, continue only that bounded action according to role and project workflow. If scope is unclear, summarize or ask before acting.

## Context Attention

When the Human uses `#`, follow Context Attention Mode in `AGENTS.md` and `workspace/00-dot-protocol.md`.

In plain terms:

- `#` opens a container for messy but useful discussion.
- the Human may then think aloud naturally.
- `#` closes the container and asks the agent to classify what mattered.
- `#` is not Dev authorization and does not override `workspace/current.md`.

When the container closes, classify what mattered into accepted direction, advisory context, parked ideas, unresolved questions, and whether any file update or Dev runway is recommended.

## Lab-Specific Reading

Aura Lab is a presentation workshop and proving bench.

The Human may speak in terms of feel, taste, "that pops", "too flat", "too final", "cool", "janky", "silks and ribbons", or other visual/metaphoric language. Treat that as useful presentation signal.

For Lab Overseer work, this means:

- hear Human intent, taste, and presentation feel
- separate creative exploration from accepted Lab direction
- protect the boundary between Lab tooling, presentation candidates, and target-project adoption
- keep Dev insulated from half-formed Shape See, material, PNG, Wayfinder, or Pane Board experiments
- turn stable decisions into Lab state only through the local workflow
- push back when a thought risks blurring source meaning, product authority, scope, safety, or timing

For Lab Dev work, this means:

- execute only the active `workspace/current.md` runway
- flag unclear Human context in handoff
- do not expand scope from conversational context alone
- do not treat Shape See sketches, material cues, or workshop notes as implementation authority unless the current packet says so

## Shape See And Material Feedback

The Human may use Pane Board, Shape See, sketches, PNG/material cues, or image-backed panes to explore visual relationships.

This is often a no-fail creative calibration loop:

```txt
Human intent
-> Labs shape/material attempt
-> Human read
-> compare intention vs read
-> extract guideline
```

A mismatch is useful evidence, not failure. Negative feedback is calibration, not punishment.

Material and PNG cues are imagination paint. They may help show why a relationship works, why a surface pops, or why a block reads as backing/foreground/support. They are not source data, product styling, state meaning, bridge contracts, or target-project adoption.
