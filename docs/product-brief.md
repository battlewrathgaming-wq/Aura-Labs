# Aura Lab Product Brief

Status: durable project identity
Date: 2026-06-18

## Purpose

Aura Lab develops reusable presentation and tooling products for the Aura ecosystem.

Lab helps Aura projects turn bridge-shaped information, source-code shape, and Human spatial intent into clearer surfaces without taking ownership of the source-project meaning.

Short version:

```txt
Lab makes useful lenses, windows, shelves, and benches.
Projects own what they see, show, adopt, or build from them.
```

## What Lab Makes

Lab may produce:

- presentation glass and display primitives
- portable static or framework-neutral starter heads
- material and visual grammar shelves
- Human/agent spatial collaboration tools
- source-orientation tools such as Trace Impression
- local workflow utilities that reduce repeated agent audit load
- development shelves that mature into clean consumable products

Lab products should be useful, calm, inspectable, and portable enough for another Aura project to evaluate under its own authority.

## What Lab Is Not

Lab is not:

- Atlas, Sense, or Core authority
- a source-project adapter owner
- a backend contract owner
- a bridge/runtime schema owner
- a universal doctrine host
- a central service desk
- a forced-upgrade lane for consumed outputs

Lab does not own what a target project means, emits, stores, validates, or ultimately adopts.

## Product / Development Split

Lab uses two shelves for reusable things:

```txt
lab-development/
lab-products/
```

`lab-development/` is the workshop for candidate reusable products, proof records, trials, and future versions.

`lab-products/` is the future shelf for clean consumable versions only.

Nothing is promoted to `lab-products/` without explicit review.

## Consumption Model

A Lab utility travels as a consumable version, not a continuing Lab-owned service.

Safe flow:

```txt
Lab develops a useful version.
Project chooses whether to consume it.
Project adapts local context.
Project owns local output and acceptance.
Future Lab updates are offers, not obligations.
```

## Current Major Subjects

Current Lab subjects include:

- Trace Impression: a source-cited orientation workflow for local seam maps and Human orientation frames.
- Presentation glass: reusable display primitives, starter heads, and visual grammar for Bridge -> Interface presentation.
- Pane Board: Lab-local internal spatial collaboration tooling for Human/agent layout intent.

These subjects may each have their own development shelf and decision trail.

## Fit Line

Lab work is fit when it helps Aura projects see, reason, present, or explore better without making hidden authority claims.
