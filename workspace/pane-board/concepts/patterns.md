## Plate-Plus-Stack With Detail Affordance

Source meaning artifact: `F:\Projects\AURA-Sense\workspace\UIUXHS18-meaning-geometry-passive-telemetry-lab-prep.md`

Surface: Sense Passive Telemetry compact readout / current-system context

Handles used: `#1/#01`, `#02`, `#05`, `#06`, `#07`

Shape:

- `#02` is a long shallow subject plate.
- `#05` and `#06` are compact activity values in a right-side stack.
- `#07` is smaller and quieter between the activity values as derived support.
- `#1/#01` wraps the settled read after the active pieces find their relationship.
- The pattern leaves room for a future quiet detail/support cue, such as a provider/sample timestamp, without turning the display into a flat row.

Human signal:

- Preferred over the tighter compact cluster because it splits the eye into areas of interest.
- Preserves long-name room for the subject.
- Keeps the activity texture compact.
- Lets the derived value play a role without driving concern.

Labs note:

- This pattern emerged after a flatter row made all values feel peer-level, and a side-stack variant cramped the subject.
- The useful correction was to keep the side stack while restoring the subject as a horizontal plate.
- Treat size as data-form scale, not production dimensions.
