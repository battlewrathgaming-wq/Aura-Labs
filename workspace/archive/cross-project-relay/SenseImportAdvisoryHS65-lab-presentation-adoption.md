## Recommended Sense Concept Starting Points

### 1. Combat Witness First-Read Polish

Best for immediate operator value.

Goal:

- make incoming DPS, repair HPS, observed balance, observed source, and observed weapon legible at a glance
- preserve observed/recent/rolling-window language

Key guardrail:

- Combat Witness must not look like historical proof or Atlas Evidence.

### 2. Passive Telemetry Compact State Strip

Best paired with Combat Witness.

Goal:

- make fresh, stale, partial, capped, blocked, degraded, and no-observation states visible without turning Passive into Threat Intel
- keep system context, kills, jumps, ratio, and sample basis scannable

Key guardrail:

- Passive Telemetry must not look like continuous target inspection.

### 3. Lane-Specific State Light Grammar

Best as a reusable Sense renderer pattern.

Goal:

- give each lane a compact, readable state signal
- distinguish blocked, stale, partial, degraded, unavailable, empty, pending, and quiet without one global state enum

Key guardrail:

- do not import Lab's neutral state labels as Sense authority when Sense lane labels are more precise.

### 4. Diagnostics Demotion Pass

Best if diagnostics are visually competing with the face.

Goal:

- keep diagnostics inspectable and grouped by lane
- keep source, freshness, basis/certainty, gaps, warnings, live IO, watcher/provider state visible
- make diagnostics quieter than primary lanes

Key guardrail:

- demotion must not hide authority or safety-relevant state.

### 5. Threat Intel / Clipboard Visual Consistency

Best after Combat + Passive first-read work.

Goal:

- make the back-page and acquisition bar visually consistent with the face
- preserve deliberate scan and short authority-window meaning

Key guardrail:

- Threat Intel must not become a front-page background monitor.
